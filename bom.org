Status values used throughout:

Every row transcribed from the manual starts at =spec=.

Board indicates which physical sub-assembly a part lives on, per the
service manual's own three parts lists:

- =Main= the main chassis board
- =Front End= AM0111(U), the FM tuner front-end module, 300-series designators
- =MPX= AM0103A, the multiplex/stereo decoder module, 500-series designators
  — not currently in use, see [[file:er-420-restoration-plan.org][restoration-plan.org]] Phase 9.

Zone filled in 2026-07-30 by walking the schematic tiles component by
component (309/378 rows). Deliberately left blank: S1 and S11 (each
spans multiple disconnected wafers across the sheet, no single cell
applies — see [[file:schematic/ZONES.org][ZONES.org]]) and other switches pending the same check.
Remaining blanks are mostly mica/ceramic caps and resistors in
corners not closely re-examined during this pass, plus a handful of
MPX-section parts — not verified against the physical unit, schematic
position only.

Notes belong in a bullet list under each table, keyed by Ref/Item —
not in a Notes cell on the row itself. A long note in a cell forces
every row's column wide, which is exactly what happened before and
made the tables unreadable.
