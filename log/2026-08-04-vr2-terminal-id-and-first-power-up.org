#+TITLE: VR2 terminal ID, speakers wired, first power-up — 2026-08-04
#+DATE: 2026-08-04
#+FILETAGS: :audio:restoration:er420:

* Goal

Pin down which physical terminal on VR2 is the wiper before trusting a DMM
sweep on it, then — if that came back clean — take the ER-420 through its
first power-up on the dim bulb tester.

* Work

Traced VR2 (500K, dual-gang loudness-tap volume pot) on schematic
tile-C5-E7 in detail: top terminal (outer leg) → C21/R33 → V3 pin 2 grid; a
separate fixed tap (not the wiper) → S4 switch → C17/C19 selectable network
(100p/0.02µF); bottom terminal (outer leg) → common ground bus shared
between both gangs; the wiper is a fourth terminal, drawn as an arrow into
the resistive element between the other two taps. Cross-checked this
against a second AI's (Gemini) reading of the same schematic — its
4-terminal identification held up once verified pixel-by-pixel against the
actual tile, but its downstream diagnostic narrative (DC leakage causing an
"always loud at zero volume" symptom) went well beyond what today's DMM
checks alone could support. One correction along the way: initially
mis-traced what looked like a wire from VR2's top terminal to switch S2 —
it was actually the D7 zone reference grid's own boundary box, not a
circuit trace. Worth remembering that zone-grid lines can look exactly
like wires at a tile boundary.

Mapped the physical clock-position lugs (10:00, 12:00, 2:00, 6:00) on the
actual pot to schematic terminals: continuity to chassis identified 10:00
as the grounded/bottom outer leg (both gangs); a 0.02µF cap in the physical
trace matched C19's value exactly, confirming 6:00 as the fixed loudness
tap; a traced wire from 2:00 to VR1 (balance pot) identified 2:00 as the
top outer leg/input; 12:00 is the wiper by elimination.

Ran the DMM 4-step test plan (total resistance, ground continuity, wiper
sweep, tap reading) on both gangs of VR2, amp unplugged. See Measurements.
[[file:../component-notes.org][component-notes.org]] updated with the
terminal ID and results — the terminal-ID section is solid, but the
Interpretation/Final Verdict sections are flagged as unconfirmed pending an
actual live voltage measurement, which nothing done today provides.

Speakers wired: CS-A31s (16Ω, screw terminals) to the ER-420's rear screw
terminals with 18AWG zip cord and U-spade connectors. Confirmed on the
schematic (tile-C10-E12) that S7 taps are 16Ω/8Ω, and set S7 to the 16Ω
position to match the CS-A31s.

First power-up on the dim bulb tester (100W bulb) — no capacitors replaced
yet, original 1966 electrolytics still in the B+ supply, so this doubled as
a first reforming test for them. Bulb went bright then dimmed down as
expected (heater warm-up and filter-cap inrush settling, not a short).
Front panel pilot lights confirmed. Added knobs to the front switches to
select stereo mode and AUX input, fed PC audio through the AUX jack. Music
played cleanly on both channels through the CS-A31s, volume control smooth.
Max volume quieter than expected — attributed to the dim bulb's voltage
drop under load (B+ sag), which is the tester working as intended, not a
fault. No smoke, no unusual smell (only incidental dust-on-bulb and
soldering-iron odor, unrelated to the amp), no arcing. Ran about 6 minutes
total before powering down.
