#+TITLE: Voltage sweep with new template and DMM — 2026-09-08
#+DATE: 2026-09-08
#+FILETAGS: :audio:restoration:er420:

* Goal

First full voltage-callout pass with the new measurements/TEMPLATE.org and
the Fluke 15B+, on the dim bulb tester. No speakers wired, S8 off, starting
on AUX.

* Work

Started with R77-R80 (the S8/speaker-off no-load question left over from
earlier sessions). Left/right identity resolved by tracing red/white wire
color from the labeled rear speaker terminal block through S8 pin 2: R79 =
red = left = T7, R80 = white = right = T8. Both measured ~16Ω isolated (S8
off), matching the printed spec.

[[file:../photos/2026-09-08-r77-r80-divider-01.jpg]]

That led to actually reading tile-D12-F12's S8 wiring directly, which
corrected the standing model: S8 is a break-before-make switch, not a
simple series on/off. Pin 1 routes the transformer tap straight to the
speaker terminal; pin 2 routes it to R79/R80 to ground. R79/R80 are only in
circuit when S8 is *off* — the opposite of what the 2026-07-29 log assumed
("taps in before S8"). circuit-notes.org and component-notes.org corrected.

Moved to C99/C100/C101 pin identification, since none of the three cans had
a confirmed per-lug mapping. C100: pin 4 = ground, pin 1 = 40µF+
screen-decoupling section, pin 2 = between R116/R117, pin 3 = R116-fed —
confirmed both by tracing and by re-measuring directly on the can pins
(within 0.2-0.3V of the resistor-lead readings). That trace also caught
circuit-notes.org's R116/R117 order being backwards — tile-G10-I12 shows
R117 upstream of R116, not the reverse. C99: pin 4 = ground, pin 2 = the
205V section (traced via continuity from V3 Pin 1 through a 100kΩ resistor
matching R38), pins 1 and 3 matched by voltage magnitude (230V and 110V
sections respectively) since the reverse assignment would put one section
above spec. "Near R81" in the old row 15 label was wrong — R81 is an
unrelated 10k resistor elsewhere; the real feed is R38. C101: "+" confirmed
at 209.4V (matches the earlier resistor-lead reading), black-dot/"-" pin is
the C101/C102 doubler midpoint (104.7V), not ground — came out exactly half
of the "+" reading, cleaner symmetry than 2026-08-15's pass.

Ran the full tube-pin sweep next (V1, V3, V4, V6-V14) against the template.
V1's first pass, taken right at the dim-bulb-settle point, showed the
classic cold-tube signature and was superseded by a second pass after 20+
minutes of warm-up (a trash-run interruption extended this well past plan).

Biggest find of the night: V12 Pin 5/6 and V14 Pin 5/6 all read ~0V, which
first looked like a possible open B+ fault. Traced V12's branch down through
a super-junction (T5 pin 4, R97, C80, one side of R101) and row 31
(R101's other side, C82+/R98/C67+) — both dead on AUX. Turned out to be
mode-dependent, not a fault: switching S1 to FM brought both nodes up to
sensible values (41.89V and 107.9V, healthy ratios against spec). Same
story for V14 on AM mode (115.1V/72.9V). AUX leaves both the AM and FM
sections completely unpowered, not just switched out of the signal path —
now documented in TEMPLATE.org so it doesn't cost a future session the same
chase.

R87 physically identified along the way (row 32's node) — NOT near V14
despite schematic-zone adjacency, actually near C99.

[[file:../photos/2026-09-09-r87-01.jpg]]

Found two part-value discrepancies against the schematic/manual, trusting
the physical markings same as R79/R80's precedent: R85 is 25kΩ (red-green-
orange-silver), not the spec'd 15k. Also noticed R91/R93 are silver-band
(±10%) against bom.org's listed ±20% — flagged but not chased further.
R101 and its neighbors turned out to be printed-text KOA-branded resistors,
not color-banded — a real, plausible-original part style, not a modern
replacement as first guessed from the printed marking alone.

Row 4 ("Between R21 and R17") got a real correction too: reading
tile-C3-E5 and tracing at the bench, it's V1's 110V bias-supply bus, not
"V1 sec. 2 cathode area" as originally guessed. R19 (2.2M) confirmed
grounded as pin 7's grid-leak resistor — its value never fit a cathode
resistor anyway.

Biggest open thread: V1 Pin 3 (section 1 cathode) read exactly 0V both
passes, with Pin 1 and Pin 6 both riding high — the classic no-current
signature, but not the cold-tube version since it held after full warm-up.
Pin identity was double-checked physically (pins 2 and 4 clearly labeled,
Pin 3 positionally between them despite its own label being obscured by
rosin) — ruling out an earlier pin-shift theory. Checked R9 (cathode
resistor) end to end tonight, from the Pin 3 socket lug through R9 to its
ground joint: 2.979kΩ, matching spec closely. That clears R9 completely,
narrowing it down to the tube itself or a grid-circuit fault at Pin 2.

R86 (row 33) was not found despite real effort — leading theory for next
time is that it's printed-text like the KOA parts near R101, not
color-banded, so hunting for bands may be the wrong search.

Changed the measurements table's Delta column to % Diff
((Measured−Spec)/Spec×100) partway through, since comparing a 0.6V cathode
spec against a 340V rail spec on the same absolute scale wasn't useful.
Once converted, a real pattern showed up by grouping rows by role rather
than table order: cathode pins sag hardest (~-50% to -57%), B+
decoupling/rectifier nodes sag moderately and consistently (~-30% to -39%),
and lightly-loaded front-end plate/screen pins barely sag at all (~-7% to
-16%) — consistent with tonight's reduced line voltage (71.6V at the
receiver's own plug, well under nominal) affecting current-dependent
cathode points hardest and lightly-loaded stages least.

Power down at the end of session: filter caps (C99, C100, C101, C102) and
all tube pins discharge-pen checked before closing up.
