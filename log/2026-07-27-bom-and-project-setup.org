* Work

Filed the Mouser receipt into =receipts/=, renamed to
=2026-07-26-mouser-recap-caps-fuses.pdf=.

Read the service manual's three parts lists (main chassis,
=PARTS LIST OF FM FRONT-END=, =PARTS LIST OF MULTIPLEX SECTION=) and
transcribed all of it into [[file:../bom.org][bom.org]] — Capacitors, Resistors,
Potentiometers, Coils/Chokes & Transformers, Tubes and Diodes,
Switches, Miscellaneous. Every row started at =Status: spec=. Added a
=Board= column (Main / Front End / MPX) so parts are traceable to
which physical sub-assembly they live on.

Reconciled Mouser order 281232819 against the shopping list and the
manual's designators: 31 capacitors plus fuses moved to
=Status: ordered= with Replacement value and Mfr PN filled in. Landed
on discrete caps underneath the original can for the C99/C100 rebuild
(47+22+22 µF, 450V) — confirmed by the order quantities matching that
approach rather than a drop-in can replacement.

Worked out the front-panel switch/pot-to-schematic-symbol mapping
across several rounds: S1 Selector, S2 Mode, S3 Tape Monitor, S4
Loudness, S5 High Filter, S6 Low Filter, S7 Speaker Impedance
Selector (rear, confirmed Glide not Slide), S8 Speaker, S10 Power
(Rotary, not Slide — corrected an assumption of mine), S11 AM
Sharp/Broad which doubles as FM AFC on the same switch. S9 is still
unidentified. VR1–VR4 (Balance/Volume/Treble/Bass) matched the manual
directly. Dropped the separate Controls section afterward — Noisy?/
Cleaned? columns now live directly on the Potentiometers and Switches
tables instead.

Simplified [[file:../bom.org][bom.org]]'s status tracking: dropped the redundant =Ord=/
=In= columns in favor of a single =Status= field (spec / keep /
ordered / installed) — Doug did the column deletion by hand with
=org-table-delete-column= and split the C99/C100 rows into one row
per discrete section rather than cramming three part numbers into one
cell.

Retired =er-420-recap-shopping-list.org= — its designator tables were
now redundant with [[file:../bom.org][bom.org]], its "before ordering" checklist was
already duplicated in [[file:../er-420-restoration-plan.org][restoration-plan.org]] Phase 2. The reasoning that
had no other home (oil paper failure mode, E12 substitution rationale,
the C99/C100 options comparison, why most of the caps are staying
original) moved into [[file:2026-07-25-parts-research.org][log/2026-07-25-parts-research.org]]. One safety
point not captured elsewhere (high voltage isn't only in the caps —
tube sockets and terminal strips too) went into [[file:../er-420-restoration-plan.org][restoration-plan.org]]'s
Standing Rules.

Turned [[file:../er-420-restoration-plan.org][restoration-plan.org]] into a living document rather than a
static checklist — decisions get recorded inline on the relevant TODO
as they're made (the C99/C100 "Chosen approach" line is the model for
this). Created open-questions.org at one point as a centralized
question tracker, then retired it again in the same session once the
better fit became clear: questions belong inline, tagged so they
surface in org-agenda, not copied into a separate file that requires
remembering to promote things into it. Its three seed entries
redistributed: the 280V cap question became a TODO here in the
parts-research log, the R77/R79/R80 discrepancy folded into
[[file:../er-420-restoration-plan.org][restoration-plan.org]]'s existing Phase 2 TODO, and the C99/C100
mounting question needed no new home since Phase 4 already asks for
it.

Gave every Phase heading in [[file:../er-420-restoration-plan.org][restoration-plan.org]] a TODO keyword and a
statistics cookie ([0/4], [0/13], etc.) so phase completion rolls up
automatically in org-agenda as sub-TODOs get marked done.

Spent a while debugging why the Phase-level TODOs weren't showing up
in org-agenda at all while the ordinary sub-TODOs were — chased a
stale restriction lock, an "e" custom command with a bad match
argument (=(todo "")= doesn't mean "all keywords" the way =alltodo=
does), and finally found the real cause: old copies of both
=er-420-recap-shopping-list.org= and =er-420-restoration-plan.org=
were sitting inside the music-map directory tree from before the
projects were split apart, and =org-agenda-files= only pointed at
music-map — so every scan was quietly reading the stale duplicates
instead of the real files here. Deleted the duplicates and set up
org-agenda properly scoped to this project (Doug ended up using a
projectile-based dynamic command rather than a static file list, so
new log files show up automatically without reloading config).

Committed everything in stages: [[file:../bom.org][bom.org]]'s initial population, the
order reconciliation + receipt + parts-research log, and
[[file:../er-420-restoration-plan.org][restoration-plan.org]] as its own commit.
