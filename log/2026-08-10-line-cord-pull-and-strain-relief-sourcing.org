#+TITLE: Line cord pulled, strain relief destroyed, cable gland sourced — 2026-08-10
#+DATE: 2026-08-10
#+FILETAGS: :audio:restoration:er420:

* Goal

Get the original 2-wire line cord out to inspect/measure the chassis strain-relief
opening ahead of the 3-wire conversion, and source a replacement strain relief sized
to fit.

* Work

Decided the evening before (2026-08-09) that pulling the cord was the natural first
step of Phase 5.1, but flagged it as a real commitment — no clean way back to a
working 2-wire cord once the interior leads are disconnected — and it was late with
poor lighting, so it got deferred overnight rather than rushed.

Attempted removal this session: the original strain-relief bushing had no visible
seam and the cord wouldn't slide within it, so the interior leads were cut (rather
than desoldered as planned) to free the cord, and the strain relief itself was
destroyed getting it out of the chassis panel. Recovered one number from the
wreckage: the bare chassis panel hole. See Measurements.

Spent the rest of the session sourcing a replacement, working through several
strain-relief styles:
- Heyco-style snap-in bushings — catalog has a gap right at the measured hole
  size (nearest standard sizes are .375" and .500", nothing at .4").
- A Mouser M16x1.5 cable gland listing — wrong on both counts: thread needs a
  ~16mm hole (would require enlarging the chassis hole significantly), and its
  2-6mm clamping range excludes the fatter of the two candidate cords anyway.
- An Adam Tech CG-P-M10-1.5 datasheet (local PDF) — strong match: recommended
  panel cutout of 10.30mm lines up closely with the measured 10.2mm chassis
  hole. Cable range 3-6mm covers the thinner "computer cord" candidate (~5.1mm)
  but excludes the 16-gauge extension cord candidate (~7.6mm) — this spec
  effectively decides which cord family gets used.
- Cross-checked a Meccanixity M10x1.5 20-pack on Amazon against the Adam Tech
  datasheet numbers rather than trusting "exact same specs" marketing language —
  thread size, thread OD, thread length, and IP68 rating all matched, cable
  range (2.5-6mm) was even slightly wider on the low end. Ordered.

* Open questions

- Chassis thickness (1mm) was measured, but not directly at the strain-relief
  hole — taken elsewhere on the chassis and assumed uniform by visual
  inspection. The Adam Tech reference part's 1.5mm max gives some margin if
  that assumption holds, but the hole itself hasn't been checked directly
  (e.g. for double thickness from a formed edge or reinforcement near the
  opening) — worth a direct check before/at install.
- Max panel thickness isn't published for the actual Meccanixity part ordered,
  only inferred from the closely-matching Adam Tech reference. Worth a sanity
  check once the part is in hand rather than assuming the reference number
  transfers exactly.
- Replacement 3-wire cord itself hasn't been sourced yet. The gland's 2.5-6mm
  clamping range rules out the 16-gauge extension cord candidate (7.6mm) and
  points toward something in the "computer cord" family (~5mm) instead — that
  choice is effectively made by the gland now, just not yet acted on.
- Which side of the fuse holder/S10 is hot was supposed to be traced before
  disconnecting the old cord (per Phase 5.1) — the leads were cut rather than
  traced/photographed first. Worth checking whether enough of the original
  dressing/routing survived intact nearby to still work this out, or whether
  it needs to come from the manual/schematic instead.
