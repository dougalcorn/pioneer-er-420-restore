* Status

Not started — blocked behind ER-420 completion. This page captures

* The speakers

Pioneer CS-A31, 1968-1970. 16Ω, 40W. 12" woofer (30-08), midrange
(MM122A), horn-loaded tweeter (PT-220). Alnico magnets throughout.

* Known condition (unverified beyond visual/listening, 2026-09-14)

- Cloth surrounds, look to be in good shape — ages much better than
  foam, no rot/crumble expected at this point if intact now.
- Sound "pretty OK" as-is, untouched.
- Have not yet done a voice-coil push test, DCR check, or opened the
  crossover.

* Plan

Recap-and-preserve, not a driver/baffle rebuild — keep the original
drivers and cabinet, replace only the aged crossover caps and
stuffing/damping. Decision driven by the condition above: no evidence
of a driver-level problem yet (healthy surrounds, DMM/push-test still
pending), so there's no reason yet to reach for a more invasive
rebuild.

Before any cap purchase: confirm the actual populated values by
opening the crossover and measuring/reading them directly — do not
just trust the reference schematic below.

** Steps (once ER-420 is done)

1. Open crossover, identify component values on the physical caps.
2. Voice-coil push test + DCR check on all drivers before touching
   anything, to rule out a driver-level issue.
3. Recap with fresh caps close to measured values (10% rule of
   thumb; parallel two caps if an exact match isn't a stock value).
4. Add internal bracing and replace stuffing (poly batting, not
   fiberglass — irritant, wear mask/gloves if removing old fiberglass
   stuffing).
5. Consider grille cloth replacement (lattice grille removal reported
   to improve SQ directly, independent of the recap).

* Cap values

Thread OP measured their CS-A31 crossover at 1.6µF and 14µF. Standard
stock values close enough (within the 10% rule of thumb): 1.5µF and
15µF.

Chosen part: Dayton Audio DMPC series, metalized polypropylene —
genuine audio-grade film dielectric, mainstream/budget tier (not
boutique). Pricing checked 2026-09-14:

- DMPC-1.5 (1.5µF, 250V): $0.79 — https://www.parts-express.com/Dayton-Audio-DMPC-1.5-1.5uF-250V-Polypropylene-Capacitor-027-412
- DMPC-15 (15µF, 250V): $4.19 — https://www.parts-express.com/Dayton-Audio-DMPC-15-15uF-250V-Polypropylene-Capacitor-027-432

~$10 total for both speakers (one of each value per speaker), before
shipping. Confirm against actual measured values before ordering —
these targets are based on the thread OP's speakers, not a direct
measurement of mine.
