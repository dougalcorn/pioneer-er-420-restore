#+TITLE: ER-420 Troubleshooting Reference
#+CATEGORY: er420
#+STARTUP: overview
#+FILETAGS: :audio:restoration:er420:

Transcribed from the service manual's own troubleshooting section
(pages 17-18). Covers the power supply and audio chain only — the
AM/FM tuner and MPX troubleshooting tables are deliberately left out,
same scope decision as the rest of this restoration.

* Power Circuit

** Does not light up or function at all

1. No power supplied.
   a. Severed power cable — check where cable enters plug.
   b. Defective power switch — check contact point.
   c. Fuse blown or no fuse inserted — replace or insert new fuse.
2. Fuse blows.
   a. Power transformer heats up — short between transformer winding
      layers.
   b. Defective insulation in OPT between primary and secondary —
      replace OPT.
   c. Short between electrodes in tubes or diodes — mainly in
      rectifier or power tube.
   d. Defective electrolytic capacitor — mainly in ripple filter
      circuit.
3. Emits smoke.
   a. Short in filament wiring — check wiring by removing tubes.
   b. Defective tube socket — mainly poor insulation of wafer-type
      socket.

** Lights up, but does not function

1. Insufficient plate voltage.
   a. Rectifier tube/diode burned out — replace tube or diode.
   b. Resistor in ripple filter circuit burned out — replace
      resistor.
   c. No rectifier.
2. No voltage at plate circuit.
   a. Primary windings of OPT burned out.

* Audio Circuit

** Does not function

1. Power amplifier section.
   a. Severed output transformer(s) windings (primary and secondary).
   b. Power output tubes damaged or heaters burned out.
   c. Plate load resistors between stages burned out.
   d. Bias resistor in cathode circuit burned out.
2. Preamplifier section.
   a. Plate load resistors between stages burned out.
   b. Poor contact(s) in input selector switch or mode switch.
   c. Defective cathode resistor.
   d. Defective de-coupling resistors between stages.
   e. Defective tubes.

** Noises

1. Tubes — defective tubes.
2. Resistors — defective plate resistors, negative feedback
   resistors, or cathode resistors.
3. Capacitors — defective capacitor in the coupling/phase
   compensation circuit.
4. Potentiometers — noise from a defective volume control or other
   variable resistor.

** Hum

1. Tubes — defective tubes, particularly a short between electrodes.
2. Resistors — defective load resistor in the phase inverter circuit,
   cathode end.
3. Capacitors — defective electrolytic capacitor in the filter
   circuit or de-coupling circuit.

** Distortion

1. Transformer — defective output transformer, output tubes, or
   rectifier (tube or diode); short between primary and secondary.
2. Capacitor — defective capacitor in the output tube bias circuit.
3. Resistor — defective grid leak resistor.
4. Rectifier — defective rectifier in the bias circuit, when a fixed
   bias circuit is employed.
